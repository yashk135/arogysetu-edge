SLIDE 3

Innovation & Uniqueness

🖼️ Slide Visuals & Layout
Headline: 4 Groundbreaking Innovations No Other Platform Has
  • 🔀 Dual-Layer Language Processing: Regional voice for patient vs 100% English 4-bullet doctor summary.
  • ⚡ Offline-First Storage Engine: Powered by client-side IndexedDB for zero-latency history.
  • 🚨 Zero-Network Emergency System: Built-in 108 emergency dialer, 2G SMS alerts with GPS, and offline first-aid protocols.
  • 📲 Omnichannel Reach: Seamless integration with Twilio WhatsApp API for direct patient access.
🎙️ Word-for-Word Pitch Script
"Why will ArogyaSetu Edge win where others fail? Because of four architectural breakthroughs:

Number One — Dual-Layer Language Processing. Standard apps translate word-for-word, which creates garbled nonsense in clinical reports. We decouple patient output from doctor output. The patient receives clear instructions in Hindi or Marathi, while the primary health center doctor gets a pristine, 4-bullet point English summary designed for immediate clinical handoff.

⚡ Number Two — Offline-First Architecture. We built the application using browser-native IndexedDB. Patient profiles, past triage history, and Health Passports load in zero milliseconds even deep inside internet dead zones.

🚨 Number Three — Zero-Network Emergency Mode. When internet drops completely in a critical emergency, our app switches to offline mode: offering pre-loaded WHO-standard first aid protocols, one-tap 108 emergency dialing, and automated 2G SMS dispatch with GPS coordinates.

Number Four — Omnichannel Integration. Patients without our app can simply send a WhatsApp message to our bot to retrieve their medical passport instantly."
🔥 WOW FACTOR: Dual-Layer Language Output — Solves local dialect communication for patients while guaranteeing standardized 100% clean English for hospital referral doctors.
⚡ OFFLINE HIGHLIGHT: 2G SMS Emergency Fallback + Offline IndexedDB Storage Engine guaranteeing zero data lockup in offline rural areas.

SLIDE 5

Technology & Architecture

🖼️ Slide Visuals & Layout
Headline: High-Performance, Resilient Technical Stack
  • Client PWA Layer: React 18 + Vite + Web Speech API + jsPDF Engine
  • Storage Layer: Browser IndexedDB (Offline Patient Database)
  • AI Intelligence Layer: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (Vision & System Instructions) + Twilio WhatsApp Gateway
🎙️ Word-for-Word Pitch Script
"Let’s take a look under the hood. Our technical stack was engineered specifically for performance under constraints.

On the frontend, we use React 18 bundled with Vite as a Progressive Web App, backed by Service Workers that pre-cache the application shell.

⚡ For storage, we bypass traditional server latency by utilizing IndexedDB directly inside the browser. Patient profiles, past triage history, and Health Passports are stored locally and synced seamlessly.

For AI processing, we integrate Google Gemini 1.5 Flash, utilizing custom system prompts engineered for deterministic triage outcomes.

To generate referral PDFs, we don't rely on slow server-side rendering. We run a client-side jsPDF compilation engine directly in browser memory. This guarantees instantaneous PDF downloads even on weak 2G networks!"
🔥 WOW FACTOR: On-Device PDF Generation Engine — PDFs compile locally inside browser JS memory in <200ms without sending data to a PDF backend server.
⚡ OFFLINE HIGHLIGHT: Complete decoupling between client storage (IndexedDB) and cloud AI endpoints.

🖼️ Slide Visuals & Layout

  • 1. 🎙️ Multi-Modal Input: Voice recording in Hindi/Marathi OR photo upload of skin lesion/report.

"This is not a slide concept — we have built a fully functional, production-ready prototype.

Step 1: The worker opens the app, selects Voice Triage or Camera AI, and inputs the patient's symptoms in Hindi or Marathi.

Step 2: The AI engine analyzes the input, instantly providing spoken audio advice for the patient and highlighting severity indicators on screen.

Step 3: At the end of the session, the worker taps 'Save to Passport'. If the patient isn't registered, they quickly enter their name and age. Our code immediately creates the profile AND migrates the entire live session history into their permanent digital record.

Step 4: The worker taps 'Download PDF'. In milliseconds, a beautifully formatted, standardized English referral document with an embedded QR code is ready for hospital handoff."
