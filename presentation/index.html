03 / 08 · Innovation & Uniqueness VibeDuo · WhyCode4U Hackathon 2026
💡 6-Feature AI Platform

"An AI doctor in every ASHA worker's pocket."

Offline · Voice-First · Camera-Powered · OCR-Smart · WhatsApp-Native · No App Store Needed

📸
Camera AI Screening
Detects anemia (nail bed), jaundice (eye sclera) & malnutrition via on-device TF.js — zero cloud.
🎤
Voice Triage Hindi/Marathi
Speak symptoms in native language. AI covers 20+ conditions — fever, TB, maternal, child, snakebite, diabetes.
📄
Report OCR Explainer
Photo a lab report → offline Tesseract OCR reads it → explains CBC values in simple Hindi. "Hemoglobin kam hai."
💊
Prescription Reader
Photograph any prescription → OCR extracts medicines, doses, timing → shows structured schedule in local language.
🏥
Health Passport — Patient Profile
Persistent patient card: name, blood group, conditions, medications, past triage history. Shareable via WhatsApp as a QR-coded report card for hospital visits. Stored offline in IndexedDB.
📱
WhatsApp Triage Bot ★ SURPRISE FEATURE
ASHA sends a nail photo on WhatsApp → bot replies: anemia risk. Sends Hindi voice note → bot replies: triage advice. Zero installation. Any phone.
⚡
Progressive Web App — Share a link. Works in Chrome. No Play Store.
ASHA gets WhatsApp link → opens in Chrome → installs in 2 seconds → works offline forever on any Android phone.

🧠 Edge AI — On-Device Inference
TF.js runs quantized MobileNet via WebGPU/WASM. 224×224px → inference in ~150ms. Tesseract.js reads reports offline. Zero server calls for basic triage.
📱 WhatsApp Bot Architecture
Twilio webhook → FastAPI → TF.js inference (server) → structured reply. ASHA sends photo/voice → bot replies in Hindi with risk + advice in <10 seconds.
🏥 ABDM Compliant Architecture
ABHA Health ID + HL7/FHIR standard. Patient records sync to India's National Digital Health Mission when internet returns.
